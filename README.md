# Melt9 AR Menu - crystal clear plate version (v25)

Test version. No brand, no colour, no logo - just a crystal
clear plate, exactly like the "Meals of 2040" picture.
Your v23 glass-box version is still saved separately as the
Promotion Food final - this v25 does NOT replace it.

## The crystal plate
- Main plate: one flat clear glass disc under the pizza,
  almost invisible (opacity 0.10). The food is the hero.
- Thin clear rim around the plate, so you can see where
  the plate ends on a light table.
- A second, larger clear plate underneath, layered like
  the glass stands in the reference picture.
- No yellow, no black base, no MELT9 logo, no brand name
  in the AR label or in the photo - dish name + price only.
- Built with flat rings only (a-ring), NOT a cylinder or
  a torus: in v24 the cylinder + yellow torus stacked into
  a solid yellow disc on the phone. A flat ring is one thin
  layer and cannot become a solid disc.
- Shadow under the pizza, 1-second flash, fixed AR label,
  Add to cart + Photo in AR - all as before, in black/white.

## How it works - iOS and Android
- iPhone: "View in your space" = Apple Quick Look.
- Android: "Scan the menu photo" = camera AR, A-Frame +
  MindAR from `libs/` (self-hosted, no CDN).
- Pizza size: `dishes.js`, `scale:` - change all 3 numbers.
- Plate size / rim: `index.html`, search "CRYSTAL PLATE".
